 Deluxe Room
 Discover the quintessence of comfort and style in our generously spacious deluxe rooms.
 We offer a total of 36 Deluxe Double/Twin rooms that are perfectly suited for couples, families with young children, and single occupancy. These rooms can easily accommodate a cot for young children. Whether you choose single or double/twin occupancy, each room features a luxurious queen-size bed and an ensuite bathroom, ensuring your utmost comfort.
 Area: 16.8 sqm (including balcony & bathroom)
 Accommodates : up to 2 adults & 1 child

 Executive Suites
 Experience the perfect combination of space and style in our executive suite, featuring a stunning view of the plantations. These rooms offer a comfortable seating area and a spacious lounge where you can relax and enjoy the scenery from your private, large balcony.
 We have a total of 18 Executive Rooms that can accommodate Double/Twin/Triple occupancy. The Executive Suites are equipped with warm wooden furnishings and have a small lounge sitting area with a closing door for extra privacy in the main bedroom and bathroom.
 They can easily accommodate up to 4 people (adults or children) with 1 double bed and 2 single beds. These room types are spacious and ideal for families with older or growing children, as well as couples and single occupancy.
 Area: 133 sqm (including balcony & bathroom)
 Available views: Mount Meru View, Sunrise & Sunset View
 Accommodates: Up to 4 adults and children

 Family Room / Interconnecting Room
 Our Family Rooms/Interconnecting Rooms are perfect for small groups of friends that wish to stay together or extended families. These rooms have connecting doors between, ensuring peace of mind for parents and a comfortable stay for families, our rooms feature a separate space for kids, providing a sense of security. The connecting door can be locked and secure from both sides. Each room has its own full option facilities including its own bathroom
