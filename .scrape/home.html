Welcome to Masailand Safari & Lodge

Welcome to the Masailand Safari Lodge in Arusha, Tanzania. Our lodge offers a prime location with stunning views of Mt. Meru, just 45 minutes from Kilimanjaro International Airport and a convenient 3-hour drive from Nairobi, Kenya.

Masailand Safari Lodge rests atop a hill, boasting nine charming cottages enveloped by a splendid natural setting adorned with lush gardens and flourishing flora. Within its confines, you’ll discover 54 exquisitely appointed rooms, including 18 luxurious Executive suites, each offering an awe-inspiring panorama from its balcony. Gaze eastward for an enchanting vista of Mount Meru and take in the sweeping Kisongo valleys sprawled across the landscape.

We are your gateway to Northern Tanzania’s safari circuit, with Mt. Kilimanjaro (116.5km), Ngorongoro Crater (191km), and the Serengeti (348km) within easy reach. Whether you’re here for business or leisure, our lodge provides a comfortable and scenic base for your adventures. Welcome to an extraordinary Tanzanian experience at the Masailand Safari Lodge.

ROOMS & SUITES

Our accommodation meticulously designed to provide you with a comfortable and convenient stay. Each room is equipped with modern amenities, including air conditioning, spacious and well-appointed bathrooms, tea and coffee makers, flat-screen televisions, seamless Wi-Fi connectivity, and a well-stocked minibar. For your peace and serenity, we’ve installed double-glazed windows to ensure a tranquil and relaxing experience during your stay.
