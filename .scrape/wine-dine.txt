 wINE & DINE
 Indulge in an exquisite culinary journey at Masailand Safari & Lodge’s restaurant, where sophistication meets the serenity of the outdoors. Our indoor and poolside dining options provide the perfect backdrop for in-house guests seeking a diverse menu that blends local flavors with international cuisine.
 Conservatory Restaurant
 Savor every bite as you take in breathtaking sunsets, casting a warm glow over your dining experience. Whether you prefer the cozy ambiance indoors or the refreshing open-air setting by the pool, we offer the perfect setting to relish a wide range of culinary delights. From local delicacies that celebrate the flavors of Tanzania to internationally inspired dishes, our menu caters to every palate. Our dedicated culinary team ensures that each dish is a masterpiece, crafted with the freshest ingredients and an artisanal touch.
