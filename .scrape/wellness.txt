WELLNESS CENTER
 Opening Hours : 
 Gym: Monday – Sunday 06:00 – 18:00
 Steam bath & Sauna: Monday – Sunday 08:00 – 18:00 (upon request and to an extra fee )
 Swimming Pool: Monday – Sunday 08:00 – 18:00
 LODGE & Garden SWIMMING POOL
 Designed for your relaxation and leisure, our pool offers a sunny retreat with comfortable loungers and towels for sunbathing. The lodge swimming pool is only available to the residents of the hotel and is open from 6:00 am to 6:00 pm, seven days a week, free of charge. Our swimming pool is perfect for refreshing while watching the sunset.
 The garden swimming pool is available to our non-resident clients for a fee and is accessible to our hotel residents free of charge. It is open from 10:00 am to 6:00 pm, seven days a week. The garden swimming pool is perfect for family outings and group gatherings, where you can enjoy and relax. It is suitable for both children and adults.
 It is located just opposite Masailand Safari and Lodge.
 MASAILAND SPA
 Discover the pleasurable world of massage therapy at our spa. Our skilled therapists specialize in various massage techniques, including deep tissue, and offer a range of treatments, from soothing facial massages to body scrub treatments and many more. Enhance your natural beauty and leave feeling refreshed, radiant, and renewed in just one visit to our spa.
 Our spa is open to the guests of the hotel from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Guests can access the steam bath and sauna, but appointments must be made at least 1 hour in advance, and charges are applicable.
 GYM
 Masailand Gym is free and accessible only to the residents of the hotel. It is open seven days a week from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. For guests who wish to start their day fresh with our fully equipped facility, we offer an extensive range of free weights and machines, including treadmills and cycling, to help you achieve your fitness goals in a dynamic and supportive environment.
